About H13

What Is H13 Steel?

H13 is a chromium-molybdenum-vanadium hot-work die steel, widely regarded as an all-rounder for its excellent hardenability, red hardness, thermal fatigue resistance and toughness. It corresponds to several international grade designations for easy cross-reference in procurement:

H13 · AISI (USA) 4Cr5MoSiV1 · GB/T 1299 (China) SKD61 · JIS (Japan) 1.2344 / X40CrMoV5-1 · DIN (Germany)

Whether you procure as Chinese grade 4Cr5MoSiV1 or specify SKD61 / 1.2344 on drawings, we can supply and process based on genuine Baosteel H13 with one complete set of inspection reports.

FAQ

H13 Die Steel — FAQ

What is H13 and which grades match it?

H13 is a chromium-molybdenum-vanadium hot-work die steel: 4Cr5MoSiV1 in Chinese GB, SKD61 in JIS and 1.2344 (X40CrMoV5-1) in DIN — combining high hardenability, good red hardness and thermal fatigue resistance.

What makes Baosteel H13 different?

Baosteel H13 is produced to Baowu Special Steel standards for better homogeneity, cleanliness and structural consistency. As an authorized distributor, we supply genuine Baosteel H13 with full quality documents.

What H13 supply forms do you offer?

Annealed round bar, forged blanks / rough-machined parts and heat-treated products — cut, forged or machined to drawings, with UT inspection and reports included.

What inspections are done before delivery?

Metallographic inspection per NADCA #207, SEP 1614 and VDG ASA covers non-metallic inclusions, grain size and annealed structure, plus tensile testing per GB/T 228.1-2021.
