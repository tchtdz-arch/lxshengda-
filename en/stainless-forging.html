Stainless Steel Forgings

Corrosion-resistant, high-strength stainless steel forgings, custom-forged, heat-treated and precision-machined to customer drawings, covering martensitic and austenitic grades such as 2Cr13, for corrosive and clean-service environments.

Stainless forgings demand uniform microstructure and surface quality. Through temperature-controlled forging and quench-and-temper, combined with UT inspection and mechanical testing, we ensure reliable service in corrosive environments.
