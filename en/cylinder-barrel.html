Hydraulic Cylinder Barrels

Large-bore hydraulic cylinder barrel forgings: forging → quench-and-temper → UT inspection → delivery, with dense structure, uniform wall thickness and reliable internal quality — a solid blank foundation for downstream finish machining.

The cylinder barrel is the pressure-bearing backbone of a hydraulic cylinder, and its internal quality determines load capacity and service life. We use forging instead of cutting from ordinary tube: continuous metal flow lines and a dense structure free from porosity and inclusions. Quench-and-temper balances strength and toughness, while UT inspection verifies internal quality. Inspection reports ship with every batch for full traceability.
