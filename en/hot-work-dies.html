Hot-Work Die Forgings

Hot-work die steel forgings: hot forging dies, extrusion die blocks, container liners, profile extrusion dies and extrusion stems forged from H13, 5CrNiMo, 5CrNi2MoV, H18, S320 and similar grades — forging → heat treatment → inspection → delivery.

Hot-work dies endure high temperature and cyclic thermal stress — the uniformity of the billet structure directly determines die life. Forging breaks up the as-cast structure, refines carbides and orients the grain flow; spheroidizing annealing produces a uniform spheroidized pearlite structure that improves machinability and reduces cracking risk during hardening; metallographic inspection (inclusions, grain size, spheroidized structure) and mechanical testing back every batch, with piece-by-piece UT inspection and reports shipped with delivery.

FAQ

Hot-Work Die Forgings — FAQ

Why must hot-work die billets be forged?

Hot-work dies endure high temperature, cyclic thermal stress and impact. Forging breaks up the coarse as-cast structure, distributes carbides finely and uniformly, and orients the grain flow — markedly improving thermal-fatigue and cracking resistance. Cast or rolled billets lack isotropic properties for large blocks, causing erratic die life.

Which applications fit H13, 5CrNiMo and 5CrNi2MoV?

H13 (4Cr5MoSiV1) offers excellent thermal-fatigue resistance — mainstream for hot forging dies and aluminum extrusion dies. 5CrNiMo stands out in toughness for hammer dies and large hot-forging dies. 5CrNi2MoV improves hardenability and overall properties over 5CrNiMo for large-section blocks. H18, S320 and other grades follow your specification; we produce to drawing and acceptance criteria.

Can dimensions and straightness be guaranteed for liners and stems?

Liners and stems are forged to drawing with reasonable machining allowance, delivered rough- or finish-machined, with stress-relief heat treatment. Tolerances, straightness and hardness ranges follow your drawing, confirmed during technical review.

What inspection documents are provided on delivery?

As standard: chemical composition, mechanical property and UT reports. For die steels, metallography (inclusions, grain size, spheroidized structure) and hardness-uniformity testing are available on request — traceable batch by batch.
